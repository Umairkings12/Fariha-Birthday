Meri Fariha,

Pata nahi tumhein kabhi yeh ehsaas hua hai ya nahi ke meri mohabbat ki yeh dastan asal mein tumhari aankhon se shuru hui thi. Woh aankhein… jinhein pehli baar dekha tha, toh shayad mujhe khud bhi nahi pata tha ke ek din yehi aankhein meri poori duniya ban jayengi.

Tumhari aankhon mein kuch aisa tha jo mujhe apni taraf kheenchta chala gaya. Pehle ek khubsurat ehsaas tha, phir pasand bani, phir aadat… aur dekhte hi dekhte mohabbat meri zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat hissa ban gayi.

Aaj meri mohabbat us mukaam tak pohanch chuki hai jahan meri soch bhi nahi pohanch sakta. Main jitna tumhare baare mein sochta hoon, utna hi mehsoos hota hai ke abhi toh tumhein samajhna shuru kiya hai. Tum mere liye sirf ek shakhs nahi ho… tum woh ehsaas ho jo mere har din mein basa hua hai.

Main tumse kitna pyar karta hoon, shayad alfaaz kabhi bayan hi na kar saken. Agar main duniya ke saare lafz bhi jama kar loon, phir bhi mere dil mein tumhare liye jo mohabbat hai, uska sirf ek chhota sa hissa hi keh paunga.

Tumhari khushi mujhe apni khushi lagti hai. Tumhari udaasi mere dil par bojh ban jaati hai. Tumhari ek muskurahat mera poora din badal deti hai. Aur tumhari khamoshi bhi mujhe hazaar baatein suna deti hai.

Toh mera jawab sirf “Tum” hoga.

Tum meri mohabbat ho, meri khushi ho, meri dua ho… aur meri woh khoobsurat dastan ho jo main chahta hoon ke kabhi khatam na ho.

With all my heart,
Umair ❤️
